task H1 of the work below. **Nothing here is built and nothing is deployed: the site's home
page is the one of 2026-11-12, and stays so until the switch of section 4 is turned on the

- **Proposed**: written here so that his decisions work; his to change. Section 8 lists the

## 1. What the owner decided

### 1.1 His words (all of 2026-11-23)

- The request: «Τα γραφικά του site πρέπει να αναβαθμιστούν ... όπως είναι μοιάζει σαν 100%
  μηχανή πρέπει να γίνει πιο κατάστημα πρόσεξε όχι e-shop ... πρέπει να προστεθούν κάποια
  στοιχεία banners βασικές κατηγορίες».
- Nothing seasonal; the showcases stand on the Recommended: «Αυτά με τα εποχικά δεν
  μπορούμε να το παρακολουθήσουμε ... Μπορείς να στηριχτείς στη μεσαία κατηγορία των
  προϊόντων στα προτεινόμενο από οικιακές συσκευές ηλεκτρονικά είδη ... μηχανήματα εργαλεία».
- The philosophy of a large store's phone page, not its copy: «δεν θέλουμε να είναι ίδιο
  θέλω να έχει την ίδια φιλοσοφία τα προϊόντα που παρουσιάζονται στα μπανεράκια δεν
  χρειάζεται να είναι τα κανονικά προϊόντα ... να ξεφύγουμε λίγο από αυτό το αυστηρό μπλε
  το οποίο είναι ψυχρό»; a darker footer with more pages, drawn from the rules, and links.
- Drawings are enough for now, and photos sit in the tile's colour: «Θα το βλεπα και έτσι
  με τα πρόχειρα σχεδιάκια... Αν μπουν εικόνες σε κάποια σημεία θα ήθελα να μπουν όπως το
  amazon μέσα στο φόντο».
- The order of the page: «Τα περισσότερο διάσημα για την αγγλία να ανέβουνε πιο ψηλά
  επίσης να ξεκινάμε με κάποιους προσφορές ... μη δίνεις τόσο πολύ έμφαση στα μηχανήματα
  και στον κήπο ... περισσότερη διασπορά ... πάνω ψηλά ψηλά άσε και μία λωριδούλα όπως το
  amazon download app».
- Google's measures, authority, simplicity: «Έλεγξε τη συμβατότητα με τα δεδομένα της
  google της αποστάσεις των κουμπιών ... πρόσθεσε περισσότερο κύρος ... δεν πρέπει να χαθεί
  η απλότητα το ότι μπαίνεις μπερδεμένος και φεύγεις αποφασισμένος».
- A tile's link, either number of its word: «Χρησιμοποίησε η ενικό ή πληθυντικό».
- One family of colours: «υπάρχουνε πάρα πολλά διαφορετικά χρώματα γίνεσαι παιδική χαρά».
- **«Κράτα αυτό και ανέβασε το»**, of the fifth proposal. Told that it is a picture and not
  yet a page, and asked three things, he answered: **«Όλα μαζί»** (it goes live only when
  the department pages, the four new pages and the phone strip are built too); **«Με
  χρυσοκίτρινο banner»** (the first banner gold, not the all-blue alternative); **«Στα
  προϊόντα της στο PicWise»** (an offer opens its products inside PicWise, not the shop).

## 2. What this does not change

- A search, its cards, their order, the Recommended, "More", the price bar, the offers on
  the cards: untouched (`PROJECT_RULES.md` 2, 3 and 5; `docs/PICWISE_DECISION_CONTRACT.md`).
- PicWise is a store's front, not a shop: no basket, no price of its own, no product page of
  its own; every purchase is on the shop's site (`PROJECT_RULES.md` 4).
- Nothing invented (`docs/ANTI_FAKE_TRUST_RULES.md`): no review, rating, discount, countdown
  or "people are viewing". A section whose data is not known is left out, never filled.
- PicWise works out no price; an offer is the shop's own words (owner, 2026-11-19).
- PicWise is never called a price comparison (owner, 2026-11-12: a heading of price
  comparison «θα κλείσουν πολλές πόρτες»). See section 8.1.
- Nothing is declared to Google (`PROJECT_RULES.md` 13): every new page carries `noindex`
  and joins no sitemap until the owner opens it; the home page keeps the `<title>` and the
  description it has ("PicWise — Find the product you want in UK shops"; the owner's words
  of 2026-10-17 in the description), its canonical address, and no structured data.
- Wording a visitor reads changes only on the owner's word, a picture first.
- Commission decides nothing here either: no order of offers, shops, kinds or banners reads
  whether the account is joined to a programme or what it pays.

## 4. The switch

**Proposed.** `data/home_store_switch.json`, written as the other switches are (`_about`,
`on`, `set`, `by`):

- `"on": false` (as built): `/` answers exactly as today, byte for byte, and every route
  this specification adds answers as a page not found. The tests that pin today's home page
  stay as they are and pass.
- `"on": true`: the home page of section 5 and the routes of the parts built.

It is not one of the preparation's files (`prepared_catalogue._FROZEN_FILES`): turning it
prepares nothing again. It is read when the site starts (a deployment restarts the site); a
test turns it for itself. Turned on, and off again, only on the owner's word.

### 5.10 What the page knows, and when

- Nothing of the page is worked out while it is drawn: the Recommended of each kind, which
  tiles have products, the offers' row and the eight shops are worked out once for each
  catalogue this process loads (the keeper's thread, after `once_loaded`, and again when
  `state.loaded_prepared_at` changes), by the search's own code (`resolve_live_search`,
  with no AI layer and nothing written to the searches' log), and kept. On a host with no
  keeper (the Vercel backup) none of it is known: the band and the offers are left out and
  the tiles are drawn unguarded.
- Advised to the builder: keep this code out of `src/picwise_providers` and
  `src/picwise_nlu`, so that the prepared file's fingerprint does not change and the
  deployment prepares nothing again.
