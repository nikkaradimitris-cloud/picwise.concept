# Application message for Awin advertisers

Owner asked (2026-10-13) what to write when applying to the large advertisers on the
shortlist. Written for UK and US programmes, so in English, and deliberately with **no
traffic numbers**: an affiliate application that overstates its audience is a false
claim on a commercial form, and a programme manager who checks will reject it and
remember. What persuades a manager of a serious programme is a clear model and a clean
ranking policy, not a number they cannot verify.

Keep it short. These are read in about twenty seconds.

---

## The message

> **PicWise — product decision engine (UK & US)**
>
> PicWise is a decision engine, not a listings or coupon site. For each buying query
> it returns exactly four curated choices and one clearly explained recommendation,
> so the visitor decides in seconds instead of browsing dozens of pages.
>
> How we rank: the recommendation is based on product rating, availability and
> retailer reliability. **Commission never influences ranking or the recommended
> choice** — this is a written rule in our product, not a preference. We publish no
> fake reviews, savings or urgency signals, and we state openly when data is missing.
>
> Why your programme: your catalogue covers [CATEGORY], one of the categories our
> engine is built for, and your product data is complete enough for an honest
> comparison — which is the main reason we select retailers.
>
> What we need: access to your product feed, including the product rating and review
> count fields where your platform provides them, as these are the first criterion our
> recommendation uses.
>
> Traffic is sent as direct clicks to your product pages through Awin tracking. We are
> early-stage and building category by category; we would rather start with a retailer
> whose data supports a fair comparison than with volume.
>
> Site: picwise.subby.cloud

---

## How to use it

- Replace `[CATEGORY]` with theirs, in their own words: "major appliances", "power
  tools and workshop", "computer hardware", "beauty and personal care", "pet supplies
  and sports". One phrase, from their own site.
- Keep the commission sentence. It is the line that separates this from the cashback
  and coupon applications a manager rejects all day, and it is true: it is in
  `docs/MISSION.md` and `docs/REVENUE_NEUTRALITY_RULES.md`.
- Keep the last paragraph. Saying "early-stage" costs nothing and buys trust; a
  manager who later finds inflated numbers closes the account.
- Add nothing about traffic, users or revenue. If a form demands a number, give the
  real one or leave it blank.

## The one line that matters most

If a form allows only a sentence or two:

> PicWise returns four curated choices and one explained recommendation per buying
> query, ranked on product rating, availability and retailer reliability — never on
> commission. We are applying because your product data is complete enough for an
> honest comparison.

## If a programme rejects you

Ask one question back, because the answer is useful either way: whether the rejection
is about traffic volume or about fit. A volume rejection is worth re-applying to in a
few months. A fit rejection means the category is wrong and no amount of waiting
changes it.

## A separate question worth asking every approved advertiser

Once approved, the feed may still arrive without ratings, as the first one did. One
message to the programme manager:

> Does your Awin product feed populate `average_rating` and the review count? Our
> recommendation uses the product rating as its first criterion, and your site shows
> review data on product pages. If those fields are not currently in the feed, is it
> possible to include them?

This is a reasonable ask, not a favour: the shop already holds the number, the column
already exists in the Awin specification, and a shop whose good ratings are visible to
a comparison engine benefits from them being there.
