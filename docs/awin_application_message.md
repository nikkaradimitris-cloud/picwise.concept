# Application message for Awin advertisers

Short and courteous. These are read in twenty seconds.

Rewritten on 2026-10-16 for the price-first plan
(`docs/picwise_price_first_plan_2026-10-15.md`). The earlier version asked shops for
ratings; PicWise no longer uses them (see the git history for the old text). What it
asks for now is what the plan needs: stock status, a full description and the
EAN/GTIN code. "Is designed to" is deliberate: the plan is not live yet, and an
advertiser who checks the site must not find a claim it does not meet.

---

> Dear [BRAND] affiliate team,
>
> Thank you for considering our application to your programme.
>
> PicWise is a product search for shoppers who already know what they want to buy. It
> is designed to show only the kind of product asked for and only what is in stock,
> cheapest first, with one recommended pick from the middle of the price range rather
> than simply the cheapest.
>
> That depends on good product data. A product appears only when its feed carries a
> clear description, and never when the feed lists it as out of stock; our recommended
> pick also needs an EAN/GTIN code. Retailers whose feeds carry full descriptions,
> up-to-date stock and product codes get the most from PicWise.
>
> Our interest is the same as yours. PicWise earns only when a visitor buys, and it
> never sends a visitor to a product listed as out of stock, so we are built to send
> you buyers rather than clicks that go nowhere.
>
> If you are happy to approve us, we would be grateful for access to your product
> feed, including the stock status and EAN/GTIN fields where your platform provides
> them.
>
> We would be delighted to answer any questions, and thank you for your time.
>
> Kind regards,
> [YOUR NAME]
> PicWise — picwise.subby.cloud

## After they approve you

> Dear [BRAND] team,
>
> Thank you for approving PicWise for your programme.
>
> One question, if I may: does your Awin product feed include, for each product, the
> stock status (`stock_status`), a full description and the EAN/GTIN code? PicWise
> needs a full description to show a product, never shows one listed as out of stock,
> and its recommended pick needs a product code, so these fields decide how many of
> your products we can show. If any of them is not currently included, would it be
> possible to add it?
>
> Thank you for your help.
>
> Kind regards,
> [YOUR NAME]
