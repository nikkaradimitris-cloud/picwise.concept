# Financial / Utility Contract Rules

## Scope

Applies to insurance, energy, banking, telecom contracts, and other term-bound utility/financial products.

## Safety Position

Picwise acts as a decision assistant/comparison shortcut.  
It does not present itself as legal, financial, or insurance professional advice.

## Mandatory Output Elements

For each choice, include where applicable:
- base cost and expected total cost framing
- key terms and duration
- inclusions and exclusions
- cancellation/exit conditions
- provider reliability signals (if available)
- user-facing risks
- unknowns and unavailable fields

## Required Transparency

Financial/utility outputs must show:
- terms
- risks
- unknowns

If information is missing, mark explicitly:
- `not_connected`
- `data_not_yet`
- `not_applicable`
- `unknown`

## Forbidden Practices

- fake savings
- fake approval odds
- fake urgency
- hiding significant fees or terms
- "guaranteed best" language when terms are variable or uncertain

## Recommendation Guardrails

- Exactly one recommended choice, with reason.
- Recommendation cannot be commission-driven.
- Recommendation reason must mention key fit factors and caveats where necessary.
