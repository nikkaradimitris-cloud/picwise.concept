# Revenue Neutrality Rules

## Principle

Revenue is an outcome of trusted decisions, not an input to decision ranking.

## Allowed Revenue Models

Picwise may monetize via:
- affiliate/referral
- lead models
- CPA/conversion models
- partner agreements

## Forbidden Revenue Behavior

Never:
- rank choices by commission amount
- hide better-fit options due to lower payout
- change recommended choice for commercial gain
- use commission score in recommendation logic

## Recommendation Integrity

Recommendation must be based on:
- user fit
- reliability
- total cost/price fit
- terms and conditions quality
- risk profile
- availability/operational viability

## Audit Requirements

Each implementation step touching ranking/recommendation must provide:
- proof of neutrality checks
- test evidence that commission fields do not affect ranking
- explicit no-fake-data verification
